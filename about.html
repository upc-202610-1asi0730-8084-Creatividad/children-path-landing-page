We are ChildrenPath !

ChildrenPath is the school transport monitoring and safety platform in Lima. It offers peace of mind for families, real-time GPS tracking, proximity alerts, and digital assistance to make school commutes safer, more efficient, and reliable.

Mission

Facilitate school transport logistics and monitoring through a secure, real-time, and intuitive digital environment that prioritizes family peace of mind and driver efficiency.

Vision

Become the leading platform and safety standard in school transportation, recognized for delivering total real-time transparency in student transit.
