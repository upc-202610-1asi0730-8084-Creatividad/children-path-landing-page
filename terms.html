Welcome to ChildrenPath, a school transport digital platform designed to connect parents, drivers, and educational institution administrators. By using this application, you accept the following terms:

1. Service Description

ChildrenPath is a tracking and coordination tool for school routes that allows: Planning route schedules and arrival/departure times; Assigning students to authorized vehicles and drivers; Registering daily attendance and itinerary changes.

2. Accounts and Roles

The platform comprises three main user profiles: • Parents: can consult the ride progress, confirm attendance, and receive alerts for their child. • Drivers: access assigned routes, student rosters, and incident notifications. • Administrators: manage users, vehicles, routes, and institutional settings.

3. GPS Tracking and Location

The platform uses GPS technology to display the current position of the vehicle and estimate arrival times. Location data is processed strictly during active routes and stored in accordance with privacy policies.

4. Student Data Processing

ChildrenPath collects basic student information required to operate the service, such as name, institution, route, and emergency contact. This information is protected by security measures and used solely to provide the service.

5. Digital Assistance

The platform includes features to log attendance, stop changes, and direct communication between parents and drivers. Records remain accessible to authorized administrators and parents.

6. Alerts and Notifications

The system sends automated notifications for: • Vehicle departure. • Arrival at stop. • Itinerary changes. • Incidents during the route.

7. Incidents

In case of delays, rerouting, or unexpected events, drivers can report incidents from the app and ChildrenPath will notify the corresponding parents and administrators.

8. Subscriptions and Payments

Platform access is provided through institutional subscriptions. Payments are processed through secure channels with receipts issued according to applicable law.

9. Privacy, Security, and Responsible Use

ChildrenPath is committed to safeguarding user privacy and complying with information security standards. Users must utilize the platform responsibly, respecting others' privacy and avoiding unauthorized uses.

10. Availability and Limitations

The platform is designed to run on compatible mobile devices and web browsers. ChildrenPath is not liable for network connectivity outages, GPS errors, or technical issues external to its infrastructure.
